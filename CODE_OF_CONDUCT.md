# Code Of Conduct

Automation Health contributors are expected to help keep the project practical, respectful, and privacy-conscious.

## Expected Behavior

- Be welcoming and considerate in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Keep feedback specific, actionable, and focused on the work.
- Respect that scheduler data can reveal private local workflows; use sanitized examples.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and read-only project boundaries.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private paths, hostnames, job names, logs, screenshots, secrets, or scheduler output.
- Repeatedly derailing issues or pull requests after maintainers have set scope.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through the repository's configured private maintainer contact when available. If no private contact is configured, open a concise issue that avoids private personal details and focuses on the moderation need.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or limit participation when needed to protect contributors and the project.
